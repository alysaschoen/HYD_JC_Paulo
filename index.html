How these numbers were built
  • All figures come from your MoeGo account, pulled 30 Sep 2026 and running through 29 Sep 2026.
  • A groom is an appointment marked Finished. Revenue is the appointment total minus tips and sales tax, so tips are on top of every revenue figure here.
  • Per-day figures divide by the days you had at least one finished groom.
  • "Booked before you leave" means the next appointment was created by the day after the groom.
  • The network comparison covers Sep 2025 to Aug 2026 and uses the 12 locations with at least 300 grooms in that window. Per-day figures are per van.
  • The dollar figures under the calculator are simple multiplication at your Q3 prices, not a forecast.
